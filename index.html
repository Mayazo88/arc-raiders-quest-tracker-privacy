Privacy Policy

ARC Raiders Quest Tracker · Last updated: August 29, 2026
ARC Raiders Quest Tracker respects your privacy. The app does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Quest progress, tracking preferences, workshop-related state, language settings, and other app preferences may be stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not transmitted to the developer.

External Data Services

The app connects to MetaForge to retrieve ARC Raiders event schedule data. Requests to MetaForge may be subject to MetaForge's own server logging and privacy practices. ARC Raiders Quest Tracker does not use this connection to create user profiles or intentionally transmit personal information.

MetaForge: metaforge.app/arc-raiders

Analytics and Advertising

The app does not currently use third-party advertising networks or analytics SDKs.

Personal Information

The app does not require registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, financial information, or other personally identifiable information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally remains on the device until it is reset within the app or the app is deleted. ARC Raiders Quest Tracker does not maintain a developer-controlled user account or remote database containing this local progress data.

Third-Party Services

Event schedule data is provided by MetaForge. MetaForge operates under its own terms and privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published at this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the support contact listed on the app's App Store page.

Unofficial App Notice

ARC Raiders Quest Tracker is an unofficial companion app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Embark Studios.
